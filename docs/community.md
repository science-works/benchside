# Why Benchside, and where it could go

Benchside helps researchers think through scientific problems and keep a private
record they can return to. It also lets willing researchers share reviewed,
generalised workflow problems so Science Works can understand what support is
missing. Sharing is optional; private research support works without it.

## Contribute a research role today

Researchers are encouraged to propose scientific role profiles, improve an
existing role, and contribute synthetic examples through a pull request to
[science-works/benchside](https://github.com/science-works/benchside).
A profile describes a scientific collaborator: its scope, useful questions,
behaviour, limits and what it records. It is not a demographic profile.
See [the contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how you or your agent can help.
Submissions are reviewed before inclusion; a PR does not automatically become
part of everyone's installed Benchside.

## Planned: researchers helping researchers

We want participation to give useful knowledge back to the research community.
The intended experience is: “Other researchers encountered a similar problem;
this workaround, software or tool helped, and here is the evidence and context.”
Over time, this could connect recurring problems, practical solutions and people
who choose to help each other.

**This community lookup, recommendation and researcher-connection service is
not built yet.** Benchside currently has no shared solution search, automatic
matching or community account system. It cannot say a suggestion comes from
another Benchside researcher unless a real, accessible source supports that.

A useful future resource should preserve attribution where contributors want
it, show the circumstances and limitations of a workaround, and distinguish a
researcher's experience from a model suggestion. Existing metascience consent
does not authorise publishing private notebooks, identifying contributors or
contacting researchers. Any new public contribution or contact feature would
need its own clear choices and review process.

Community reference corpora and search projects such as
[XERJ](https://github.com/xerj-org/xerj) are useful inspiration. XERJ is not a
Benchside dependency or a selected backend, and this roadmap is not a claim
that its features are already integrated.
